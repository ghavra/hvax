Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Data Handling

BidEyes processes confidential bid documents. Documents uploaded for analysis are processed via the Anthropic Claude API. We do not retain your bid documents on our servers longer than 30 days post-processing.

Anthropic's Commercial Terms

As an API customer, Anthropic does not train their models on our customers' proprietary data.

Data Security

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.

Files are stored securely during processing, kept only as long as needed for review, accessible only by authorized personnel, deleted immediately upon request, and an NDA is available.
