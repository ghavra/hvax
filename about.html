About HVAX

De-risking the bid desk.

HVAX is an AI software company dedicated to building specialized tools for difficult, information-heavy business workflows. Our flagship product, BidEyes, is designed specifically for commercial preconstruction.

The Founder

Raghav Sharma

Founder, HVAX

I built BidEyes after seeing how much risk commercial electrical estimators carry on every bid due to compressed timelines and massive document sets. BidEyes acts as a tireless second set of eyes, surfacing hidden scope gaps and contradictions before the bid goes out.

Disambiguation & Legal Entity Notice

HVAX is a an early-stage software project based in Delhi, India. We are not affiliated in any way with HVAX Technologies Limited, an NSE-listed turnkey cleanroom and HVAC equipment company founded in 2010. Our exclusive focus is building preconstruction software (BidEyes) for electrical contractors.
