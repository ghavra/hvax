About BidEyes

BidEyes is pre-bid risk review software for commercial electrical subcontractors. It is built and run by Raghav Sharma.

Legal status

HVAX is the trading name of a sole proprietorship owned and operated by Raghav Sharma. It is not incorporated and does not have a company registration number (CIN). Any agreement for BidEyes, including pilots, is with Raghav Sharma trading as HVAX.

What BidEyes does

BidEyes helps commercial electrical estimators review bid packages—ITBs, drawings, specifications, addenda and alternates—to surface potential scope gaps and conflicts before the bid goes out. Each finding cites the exact page it was found on.

Who builds it

Raghav Sharma founded BidEyes in March 2026 and builds, runs and supports it personally.

How AI is used

BidEyes uses the Claude API from Anthropic to assist with evidence extraction and drafting. It does not replace estimating judgment, code review or contractual advice.
