What BidEyes does

Estimators upload drawings, specifications and addenda. BidEyes surfaces potential scope gaps and conflicts, such as a mismatch between a drawing and a spec section, each with a citation to the source page, so the estimator can check it before the bid goes out.

Who builds it

Raghav Sharma founded BidEyes in March 2026 and builds, runs and supports it personally. When you write to the founder address, he reads and answers it.

How AI is used

BidEyes uses the Claude API from Anthropic to assist with evidence extraction and drafting. It does not replace estimating judgment, code review or contractual advice.
