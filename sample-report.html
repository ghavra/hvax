Addenda Delta: The pattern worth noticing

Two of the four Priority 1 findings exist because an addendum changed the answer but the base document was not updated.

Addendum 1 removed theatre lighting, yet the scope list still carries the fixture package. Addendum 2 narrowed the pre-purchase boundary in an RFI answer, yet the scope text still carries the catch-all. We don't just read the bid package. We reconcile changes across the issued documents.

Sample finding, as delivered

Every finding follows the same shape: source, issue, impact, action, with the exact quote, document and page.

P1 #01 Temporary power: $150,000 appears twice BEFORE PRICING
What Changes
PRICE RFI
Source Project Manual p. 206 (§002226.01 item 17.a) and p. 218 (3.02.D.3.a.2)
Issue The package states $150,000 for temporary power in two locations with different wording. The second sentence ends without saying what the money is for.
Impact If both are the same allowance and you carry it twice, you are $150,000 high. If they are two allowances and you carry it once, you are $150,000 low.
Action RFI before pricing. Confirm whether the two $150,000 figures are one allowance or two, and what the second covers.

"Include an amount of One Hundred Fifty Thousand ($150,000) for power usage costs. Any unused portion of these funds will be credited to the Owner"

§002226.01 item 17.a · Project Manual p. 206

"Include an allowance of $150,000 in this Contractor's bid for temporary power"
