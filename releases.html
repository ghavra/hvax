Release History

A log of updates, new features, and improvements to the BidEyes review pipeline.

October 10, 2026

Private Beta Authentication & Concierge Onboarding

Implemented secure Clerk authentication for all pilot users. Because we handle highly confidential estimating data, we have disabled automated self-serve uploads. Upon account creation, users now enter a pending status while our engineering team manually provisions dedicated, secure workspaces and executes NDAs.

October 5, 2026

Added Exact-Page Citation View

Estimators need to verify AI findings instantly. We updated the risk dashboard to explicitly cite the exact source document, page number, and verbatim clause for every identified conflict, eliminating the need to search through 900-page MEP manuals.

September 28, 2026

Division 1 Cross-Referencing Logic

Enhanced the pipeline's ability to detect conflicts between Division 01 (General Requirements) and Division 26 (Electrical). The system now reliably flags discrepancies in Temporary Power allowances and shutdown notice periods.

September 15, 2026

Initial Pipeline & Clark Electric Pilot

Successfully ran our first end-to-end extraction pipeline on an 888-page middle school project for Clark Electric. Surfaced 4 Priority-1 risks and drafted 8 RFI-ready questions.
