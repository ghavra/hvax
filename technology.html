Technology

Powered by Anthropic Claude

BidEyes is built from the ground up on Claude to handle massive bid packages that break traditional chunk-based AI models.

Why Claude?

Commercial construction bid packages aren't just long; they are deeply interconnected. A note on a Division 26 drawing on page 850 might contradict a mechanical specification on page 120. Standard retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) models chop these documents into chunks, completely destroying the context required to spot these contradictions.

We chose Claude (by Anthropic) specifically for its industry-leading long-context window. By feeding the entire 1,700+ page bid package into Claude simultaneously, BidEyes maintains perfect cross-document awareness. Claude can trace a scope requirement from the Invitation to Bid, through the General Conditions, directly into the Electrical drawings.

The 5-Step Review Pipeline

  1. Ingestion: You upload the complete package (ITBs, specs, drawings, addenda).
  2. OCR & Parsing: We normalize the unstructured text and vector drawings.
  3. Full-Context Loading: The entire set is loaded into Claude's context window.
  4. Domain Reasoning: Using specialized preconstruction prompts, Claude analyzes the package for electrical-specific risks, scope gaps, and contradictions.
  5. Source Faithfulness: Claude cites the exact document and page number for every finding.

Human-in-the-Loop

BidEyes is an assistive intelligence tool. Claude flags the risks, prioritizes the contradictions, and drafts the RFIs, but the estimator always makes the final call. Our UI allows preconstruction teams to review Claude's findings side-by-side with the source documents before finalizing their numbers.

Data Handling & Security

Your proprietary bid documents remain yours. Through our API partnership with Anthropic, Claude does not train on your data. Documents are processed securely, analyzed, and then purged from our active processing servers. We maintain strict isolation between customer workspaces.

What's Next

As Anthropic continues to push the boundaries of Claude's reasoning capabilities, BidEyes is evolving to support even larger multi-trade packages, automated quantity takeoff validation, and deeper historical addenda tracking.
