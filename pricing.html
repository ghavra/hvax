Common questions

What counts as one bid?

One active bid package—regardless of page count or number of addenda. A 200-page tenant improvement and a 1,000-page hospital both count as one bid.

What if I bid more than 4 per month?

Email us. We’ll work out a volume rate. Some months are heavy; we’ll flex with you rather than cut you off mid-cycle.

Is there a contract?

Month-to-month. No annual commitment required. If HVAX doesn’t pay for itself in the first 60 days, cancel—no questions asked.

Can I try it before committing?

Yes. Send us one active or recent bid package and we’ll run a sample review so you can judge the output before subscribing.
